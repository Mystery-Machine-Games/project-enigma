## Vision Statement

You manage a repair shop and must solve puzzles to fix weird machines and increase your rating. Mechanics include NPC dialogue, picking up and placing objects, reassembling torn manual pages, diagnosing and repairing machines, and gaining reputation. Low-poly aesthetic, inspired by *WEBFISHING*.
